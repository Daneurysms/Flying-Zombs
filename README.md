The game is about clicking zombies who have mutated to a point where they can fly. Clicking on them makes them teleport to another location.
Clicking at more zombies gives more points and there is a 30 second timer.
The way to play it is pretty simple. Click on the zombies using your mouse and you get one point, but the zombie has now teleported to a new location. The more times you click on the zombies, the more points you earn.
